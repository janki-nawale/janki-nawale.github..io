Hi, I’m Janki.

I’m a linguistic researcher and multilingual AI strategist at AI4Bharat, IIT Madras, working at the intersection of language, technology, and culture.

My work brings together researchers, engineers, and language communities to build and evaluate AI systems that are more inclusive, equitable, and representative of India’s linguistic diversity. From speech recognition and machine translation to LLM fairness and AI safety, I’m interested in making technology work better for the people and communities it serves.

With a Master’s degree in Linguistics and a Bachelor’s degree in Foreign Language, Literature and Culture from Jawaharlal Nehru University, I’m fascinated by how language shapes the way we think, express ourselves, and understand the world—and how these connections can inform more thoughtful, culturally grounded AI.

Beyond work, I’m usually learning a new language, painting landscapes, reading fiction and social realism, or finding inspiration in new places and unfamiliar cultures.

Projects & experience

Project · Apr 2024–Mar 2025

FairLLM / INDIC-BIAS ↗

I led the development of INDIC-BIAS, an India-centric benchmark evaluating fairness across 85 identity groups, including caste, religion, region, and tribe. I evaluated language models for context-specific stereotypes and helped release the benchmark as an open research resource.

Research collaboration · Nov 2024

IMDA Singapore AI Safety Red-Teaming Challenge ↗

I designed and ran benign-user prompts in English and regional languages to probe cultural and linguistic bias in language models. With 50+ experts from nine Asia-Pacific countries, I contributed to a multilingual cultural-bias taxonomy and reported model failures in regional language and cultural contexts. Our team placed second.

Project · Jan 2022–2024

IndicASR / IndicVoices ↗

I led development of INDICVOICES, a 7,348-hour speech dataset spanning 22 languages, 145 districts, and 16,000+ speakers. I designed collection protocols, transcription and annotation guidance, and quality workflows, and coordinated with NGOs, universities, and collection teams across India. I also helped develop benchmarks covering 19 Indian accents.

Project · Dec 2022–2023

IndicTrans / IndicTrans2 ↗

I contributed to the Bharat Parallel Corpus Collection, including data curation and quality assurance for a 230M-pair multilingual corpus across 22 Indian languages. I helped ensure linguistic accuracy, domain diversity, and cultural relevance.

Policy research · Aug–Dec 2025

AI Policy Clinic · Center for AI and Digital Policy ↗

I tracked global AI policy developments and researched national AI policies and instruments across India, Sweden, Switzerland, and Latin America. I also contributed research and drafting to the AI and Democratic Values Index.

Project · Apr 2025–ongoing

BodhiSetu — Multimodal Cultural LLM ↗

I lead linguistic design and community-driven data collection to document underrepresented Indian cultural heritage across six languages. I developed multilingual annotation, quality assurance, and knowledge enrichment workflows for a repository of 7,000+ cultural entities.
